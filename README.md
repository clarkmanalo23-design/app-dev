# Mr. Robot

**Mr. Robot** is a critically acclaimed cyber-thriller series created by *Sam Esmail*. Even though it is a TV series, every episode is shot with the cinematic quality of a feature film[^1]. 

> "Hello, friend. That's lame. Maybe I should give you a name, but that's a slippery slope. You're only in my head. We have to remember that." — Elliot Alderson

---

## Plot Summary

The story follows **Elliot Alderson**, a brilliant cybersecurity engineer by day and a vigilante hacker by night. He is recruited by a mysterious insurrectionary anarchist known as "Mr. Robot" to join a group of hacktivists called `fsociety`. 

Their ultimate goal is to start a revolution by erasing all consumer debt held by the largest conglomerate in the world, ~~E Corp~~ **Evil Corp**. It is a ==very important== show for anyone interested in technology and psychology.
